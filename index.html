Interference field

Pressure amplitude · blue = cancelled, amber = reinforced
Destructive (quiet) Neutral Constructive (loud)
Each pixel shows the steady-state amplitude of A·sin(ωt − k·r_A) + B·sin(ωt − k·r_B + φ), with 1/r spreading loss. Dark curves are nodal lines — places where the two waves arrive exactly out of phase and cancel. This is why real ANC only works in a small zone around your ear: move half a wavelength and a null becomes a peak.

Sampling

Why it matters

Nyquist: a sample rate of fs can only represent frequencies below fs/2. Anything above folds back down and reappears as a different, lower frequency that was never there.

Drag the signal above half the sample rate and watch the reconstructed curve (amber) stop following the true wave (grey). That impostor frequency is aliasing, and it is why every distortion plugin oversamples: clipping generates harmonics far above Nyquist which would otherwise fold back as inharmonic mush.

Bit depth sets the noise floor: roughly 6.02 × bits dB of dynamic range. Drop it to 4 bits and the staircase is plainly visible.
